#mechanics

As a way to slow moments down and give everyone a chance to talk or react any player can move to open a "turn-based" speed/mode.

In this mode, the DM preserves the right the deny and restrict questions.

***
Everyone will be given:

- 1x Dialogue Response
- 1x Action
- 1x Bonus Action
- Character-Specific standard of movement

***

"Turn-Based Mode" will end as soon as the event reaches its' end as determined by the DM.
