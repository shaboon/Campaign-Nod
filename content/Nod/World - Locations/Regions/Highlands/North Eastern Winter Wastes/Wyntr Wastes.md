**Background:**

North Eastern Winter Wastes is one of the [[Regions]] of Nod

An nearly eternally snow buried region. Cold summers and frozen winters makes life away from the lake and river nearly impossible. The thick forests confuse travelers constantly. This region is only for those that want to go missing, or want death.
