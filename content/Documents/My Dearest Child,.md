
I have always supported your life and the choices you make. Know that I continue to do so from entirety of my heart.

I have been made aware of your escapades in the now destroyed Farru. I cannot know what actions led you this way, but I believe that you did what was best for the world.

That said, for the good of the family name, I must denounce you as my child and heir to the Flowers name. This is not something done with ease, or by my own hand. For if I were to continue to carry your actions in the same breath as our family, we would lose everything.

I send this letter to you letting you know that you are welcome in our home, but our public relations must be kept at minimum if any at all. This means that any influence you had with our nobility is now lost, and our funds can no longer sustain you, as demanded by the Council.

I love you my boy, change the world and when the moment proves right - I will stand next to you in arms.

Please burn this letter once you finish reading it to maintain secrecy.

~ From someone who holds you dear
