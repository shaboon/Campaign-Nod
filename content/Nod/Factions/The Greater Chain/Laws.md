
Rules put into place by [[The Greater Chain]] and enforced by the guard.

While each settlement is allowed their own legislation and local enforcement, The Greater Chain enforces laws that apply over the entire continent of Nod. 

===
Section 1 (§1) Theft and Property
---
1a. All thieving is punishable by the owner of the stolen goods, and the accused is subject to restraints via the local guard.
	1b. Should the accused be found stealing property of The Greater Chain or its 
	affiliates, local jurisdiction surrenders control of the investigation and the accused.

2a. Hiding stolen items or harboring stolen goods is punishable by law, reasonable punishment extending to a public lashing or 3 days spent in public stocks.

3a. All citizens hold the right to their own property and control of their own civilian presence. By notary of a public official, an individual may be formally commanded to leave the premise as defined as a trespass.

===
Section 2 (§2): Assault and Terror
---
1a. Whoever commits a violent act against another, physical or verbal, is subject to imprisonment and compensation for local wellness costs.
	1b. Should the subject of a violent act be a member of the governing body, punishment will include fines equal to the damages inflicted as well as the surrender of child or blood.

2a. Individuals causing undo unrest or public panic may be subject to home arrest with S35a sound ordinance restrictions.

3a. Stalking, defined as the unreasonable fear instigated by continual harassment by an individual and/or their associates is punishable by 5 days spent in public stocks.

4a. All unverified speech and claims against The Greater Chain and its representatives is subject to review. During investigation, all speech and claimants are subject to silence. Any individuals found in breach of this stipulation may be subjected to imprisonment.

===

Section 3 (§3): Sound Ordinance
---

Unlawful Speech and Sound:
	U-Sp: Defined as speech outwardly false or misleading.
		(a). Defaming: False or provably inaccurate public claims or statements in regard to a person or entity.
		(b). Disturbing: Speech found to be upsetting or disruptive to local peace.
		(c). Upsettingly Loud: Unpermitted verbal noise found to be disruptive to persons and activity of the local area.
	U-So: 
		(a). Indecent: Sounds related to intimate or private activity
		(b). Disturbing: Sound found to be unsettling or uncomfortable and disrupting local peace.
		(c). Upsettingly Loud: Unpermitted non-verbal noise found to be disruptive to persons and activity of the local area.

1a. All approved speech and sounds are permissible
	1b. Should substantial complaints be pressed against, the sound ordinance restrictions may be increased depending on severity.

2a. All approved speech and sound is permissible within a 50units radius
	2b. Breach of the sound ordinance can be punished by either/both fines amounting up to 100g and increased sound ordinance restrictions.

3a. All approved speech and sound is permissible within the bounds of the property 10units from outer structure walls/barriers.
	3b. Breach of the sound ordinance can be punished by either/both fines amounting up to 150g and increased sound ordinance restrictions.

4a. Speech and sound permissible within 20units from outer structure walls/barriers in the presence of assigned noise officer.
	4b. Breach of the sound ordinance can be punished by either/both fines amounting up to 200g and increased sound ordinance restrictions.

5a. No sound of any kind may be heard from outside the immediate walls/barriers of affected individual's property or residing structure.
	5b. Breach of §5-5a is punishable by imprisonment, and fines of 250g

===

Section 4 (§4): Substance and Property
---

1a. Any flesh from a sentient being, as defined as able to speak and understand language, is considered prohibited. Possession and/or consumption of such materials is punishable at minimum by indefinite imprisonment and at maximum public execution of self and lineage.

2a. Enhancers of any kind must be prescribed by an official, else any procession on person beyond 400mg, and 2000mg on property is considered unlawful possession subject to immediate seizure and search of all properties. 
		Fines start at 1g per 1mg.
	2b. Enhancers of any kind must be prescribed by an official, else any procession on person beyond 400mL, and 2000mL on property is considered unlawful possession subject to immediate seizure and search of all properties. 
	Fines start at 0.5g per 1mL.

3a. Controlled substances found on person or property beyond single prescribed dosage is considered questionable, and individuals found of such may be subject to searches of person and property.

4a. Encroachment of property by means of structure or foliage is subject to seizure or modification by the offended individual. Exceptions include natural growth from longstanding foliage, water and/or natural liquids, structures made by non-sentient beings/entities. 

Section 5 (§5): Decency and Public Display
---

1a. Improper clothing of one's self within a public setting as defined as exposure of coverable genitalia.

2a. Offensive or Derogatory visuals worn on body or property may be subject to confiscation and imprisonment.
	2b. Should resistance be found in the removal and/or review of relevant materials, the offender may be subject forceful imprisonment by any means at the location and any areas of which they may enter.
