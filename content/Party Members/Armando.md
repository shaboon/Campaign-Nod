Name: Armando Flowers

Race: Armadillo

Class: Oathbreaker Paladin

***

Home Region: Eco-Desert
Hometown: Darren

Background:

	Armando Armadillo was born to a life of wealth and luxury, heir to the Armadillo Sugar Plantation, he received the best education, training, and pleasures available, growing to become quite the arrogant and entitled youth in the process. This all changed however when he met the beautiful philantopist, Carmen Capybara, heiress of the Capybara Coconut Farms. Unimpressed by his wealth and charm, the haughty rich girl frustrated Armando to no end as he struggled to understand why she would not be enthralled by his presence and why she would care about the pesants. On a whim, Armando began spending his free time volunteering and helping the needy, befriending an orpahn named Cid, initially out of curiosity, but soon out of his own sense of duty, he came to care for the people he was helping, the orphaned children, the lonely widows, the homeless and infirm. Without even truly realizing it his heart began to change, as did Carmen's. Volunteering together, he and Carmen eventually became good friends, and soon afterwards, fell for one another. It was not meant to be it seems, however, as when their families found out they banned them both from seeing one another, citing a long standing historical dispute between their ancestors. To keep them apart, they sent Carmen far away, leaving Armando broken hearted.

	Six months have passed since Carmen was spirited away, Armando, after wallowing for months in depression, he realized Carmen would not want him to continue in this state. Appealing to family tradition, he asked his father for the ancient family armor which they used to conquer the land they now live on, so that he may go out on a quest. His father, while reluctant, permitted him to go on his journey if it would cheer him up. Thus, Armando donned the family armor, not for conquest, but for loves sake, for the love of his beloved, and for the love of his fellow critters.
