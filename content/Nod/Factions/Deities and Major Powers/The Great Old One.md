**Background:** 

Deeply Reknown - Scry’Laeh Enfiel, the leader of the Tytonidae clan, spent most of their life gazing upon the stars. Having perceived the greater worlds beyond them, they were the first to touch the arcane. Having their feathers painted with the colors of night - they take on new students only when their previous students pass on. Unlike other patrons, their power was not given to them by a single deity, but seemingly discovered on their own and then empowered by the Great Tree Urhn itself.
