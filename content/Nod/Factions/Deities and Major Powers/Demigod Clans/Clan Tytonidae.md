**Background:**

A reclusive group that has sat on the verge of ascension for eons.

They have overseen ages, and have influenced several powers of the years.

Having spent ages leading powers, they have spent an equal time in seclusion.

In current age, the Tytonidae have returned to reclusion. Killing those they deem trespassers.

Eternal enemy of [[Clan Corvae]].
