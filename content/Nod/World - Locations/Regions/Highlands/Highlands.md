**Background:**
Highlands is one of the [[Regions]] of Nod

With the expansion of the Lizardfolk, the trade demands of [[Clan Muridae]] have increased tenfold. However, in line with the expansion of other clans, their farmland has been consumed by population growth and accommodations. In line with the dark mushroom appearance, much of their farmland suffers from a similar disease—decreased crop quality and yield. Despite being the ones who grow the food, they are also the first to starve.
