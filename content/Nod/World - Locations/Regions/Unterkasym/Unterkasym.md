**Background:**
The Underground [[Regions]] of Nod that encompasses the entire continent.

One would imagine that the continent and surrounding islands would be as they are above land as they are below - but that is not the case for those who dwell underground. For them, all lands are connected in the dark and soft glow of the earth. Matching their world view, their cities are all vastly connected and even reach lands seemingly divided by water (Construction of such pathways thanks to the engineering ingenuity of [[Clan Cheloni]]). Being the experts of bountiful treasures below the trees and herbs, they alone control the trade of ores and precious stones. Yet, you rarely find greedy individuals here. For most of their denizens seemingly lack greater aspirations, finding their own bounty with what they already have.
