**Population Majority:** Avian

**Council Representative:** [[Counselor Armarae]]

***

**Background:**

Since [[Clan Lacertilla]] and Clan Cheloni were backed by Muridae as their own representatives, Clan Passeri had been forced to obey and respect all borders. Being denied access to every region without restriction has had its toll on Clan Passeri’s arcane developments. As such, Counselor Armarae has enacted tariffs on all foreign goods as well as a restriction on access to any of their libraries. Now, all denizens must have a valid pass to enter such places. For Anura, Licertilia, and Chelonii - this process of “material preservation” has not inhibited their people. Clan Muridae has had no shortage of stoppages and obstacles approving access.
