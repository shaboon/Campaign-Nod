**Background:**

Living somewhere where all of nature meets, the Archfey observes the world around them as an outsider. 

Despite their unassuming presence, they have been known to strike down those they feel threaten their ‘grove’ with what can only be described as nature’s fury. Funnily enough, some report that their legendary greatclub has since been retired, some even saying it’s been missing from their grasp for over 20 years.
