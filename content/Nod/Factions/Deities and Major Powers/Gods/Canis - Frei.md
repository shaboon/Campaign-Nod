**Background:**

*The Story of Frei*

At the time of ascension, it was told that the Canis clan gathered within the forests of Wynter Bane.

In their circle sat a sapling of the Great Tree Uhrn, and with full-moon's light it spoke to them.

"I grant thee god-hood, Canis clan"
"By virtue of hunt and family, you are granted immortality and bearing on the world order."

Each leader of every pack seemed to agree that this was their best step forward. A way to enhance their abilities and bring forth change. Become a figure of a new age where families acted as one, and pursued goals as a unit. 

That is, till one young pup stepped forward. "This godhood we are promised. Doesn't it defeat our own very nature?" "In immortality, we leave behind the very cycle we help sustain." "If we are to act as custodians of this realm, we cannot ascend to a new plane." "To enhance our abilities, we must face the threat of our own lifespans. " 

A chorus of murmurs resonated through the trees as the families began questioning their decision. Jörn, the Frei's father then beckoned, "Young pup, it is the cycle of all other creatures that we ascend. That we may open the fields we hunt to our prey.". 

The pup takes a step back as he considers his next words. "But as a deity we would be giving up our connections to each other. If family is something we represent, then how does a singular god show that to everyone else? Our individuality makes our unity significant." 

With Frei's last question, a shudder reverberates through the branches of the sapling. 

"Young one, your questions will not go unheard. Your tribe shall ascend, and you shall remain. Your pilgrimage shall give you the answers you seek." 

It was then said that all of Canis then joined the stars and on every full-moon you can hear the wind call for Frei, and if you find yourself near Oblivion, you can hear her longing cry.
