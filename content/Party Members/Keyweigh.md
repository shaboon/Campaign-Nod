Name: Keyweigh

Race: Kiwi

Class: Druid / Barbarian

***

Home Region: [[Highlands]]
Hometown: [[Forests of Celemor]]

Background:

	Originally from the accompanying Celemor, Kyweih was driven away for his unusual appearance and magical disposition.

	Kyweih was forced to survive in the forest from a young age. The local Corvids grew to like his demure and raised him as one of their own. Over countless years, he lived off the land, and his Corvid patrons would help give him tips to survive and cared for him.

	Due to being ousted, Kyweih does not trust city folk. He often keeps to himself and only converse with denizens of the forest. He is fiercely loyal to his Corvid supporters.
