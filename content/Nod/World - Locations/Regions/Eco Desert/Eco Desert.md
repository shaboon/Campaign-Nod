Background:
Eco Desert is one of the [[Regions]] of Nod

The Lizardfolk have faced backlash recently due to their quick expansion. Clan Muridae and [[Clan Lacertilla]] have faced numerous accounts of territorial qualms due to said activity. Many other clans see the Lizardfolk as acting out of nature by granting themselves artificial resources.  The proverbial straw that broke the camel god’s back was the most recent expansion in the Highlands. Not only did Clan Lacertilia make an unfounded claim of land in Castor territory, but it was an even further wound to see Clan Passeri share their arcane secrets with Clan Lacertilia. Being so secretive in the past, this act of grace has left most to suspect this was done to spite Clan Muridae.
