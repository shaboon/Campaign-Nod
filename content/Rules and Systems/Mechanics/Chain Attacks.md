#mechanics 

	Activation: 
When party strikes an enemy that is stunned or prone, they can initiate a chain attack

***

	Effect:

Damage: Players will roll for whatever damage/attack they'd like. Any magic used can be done for free - BUT MUST BE LEVEL 1 or LOWER  
Cycles: Cycle will be started with the initiating player and will go down the line of initiative  
First 2 cycles are free - after each cycle, DM rolls a D20 and must roll higher than the initiating player’s initiative roll. After each subsequent cycle, add a +(n) to the cycle breaking roll  
  
Once Chain Attack is broken, all players roll a D20 - if any of them are within 2 digits of the rolled Chain breaking number, all players get to roll for damage. Regardless of result, players will return back to normal combat  
  
The subject of the chain attack will be immediately absolved of their condition (Stunned/Prone)
