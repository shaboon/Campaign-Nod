**Background:**

Clan Castor, in [[Celemor]], have closed their borders following the dam 
failure in [[The Old Basin]]. This occurred merely days before the appearance of the dark fungus. 
Now, black water flows endlessly from their territory, flooding what remained of Heartwood and the Rootlets.
