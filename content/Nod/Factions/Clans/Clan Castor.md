***

**Region:** [[Highlands]]

**Population Majority:** [[Clan Castor]]

**Council Representative:** [[Counselor Caedric]]

***

**Background:**

Representative of all beavers.

A proud and slightly supremest group known for their engineering prowess. Hired and utilized by [[The Greater Chain]], they are responsible for the the creation of [[Farru]].

Given their history, the Castors are the go-to architects of Nod. 

Having done so much for Nod, it is natural that the Castors were given control [[Celemor]] Dams.
