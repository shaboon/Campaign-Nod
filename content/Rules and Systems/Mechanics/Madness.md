#mechanics

Sources mainly include [[Stagnation's Breath]].

The status works in a scale of 0-20 points.

Whenever the status is inflicted, the affected character starts at 10 unless specified by DM.
Interactions with characters may result in d4 dice rolls either in favor or against the character's current condition.

Ex. +d4 / -d4

***

When a character reaches:

- 5: The character is much more anxious and physically unwell
- 10: The character is in a nominal state, and react normally
- 15: The character may feel much more relaxed and resistant to anxiety

***

Once a character hits 20:
They are absolved of the condition with resistance to Madness until longrest.
