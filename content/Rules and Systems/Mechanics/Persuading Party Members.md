#mechanics 

	Activation: Players disagree on an action.

***

	To convince a party member, make an argument or proposal. The players will then use rolls as decided by the DM for the situation to determine if the player character convinces their party member by beating their saving throw. Keep in mind, rolling a nat1 can have unexpected consequences.
	
	Ex1. Gilfry wants Lyl to cook the soup this time, Lyl rolls a 16 with a +1 for Charisma saving throw (used this time as it is a conversation of words). Gilfry rolls a 17. Lyl is convinced to make the soup this time
	Ex2. Barf wants Bingus to open an obviously trapped door. Bingus rolls a 10 -2 for Charisma saving throw. Barf rolls a nat1. In trying to convince Bingus, Barf somehow convinces themself to open the door instead, as such they are launched at mach 20 within the next 5 seconds.
	Ex3. Juliet wants to convince Romeo that shes a better fighter than him. Romeo rolls a 15 +0 Charisma Saving throw. Juliet throws a nat20 Strength roll. She uses Strength here instead as she has decided to convince him, she wanted to lift Romeo up and pin him against the wall. Romeo was convinced.
