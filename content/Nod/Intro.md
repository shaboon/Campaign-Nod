
You find yourself in the cold embrace of the void. As you gaze around your surroundings, all you see is darkness…

But you are not alone, something is shifting…perhaps even writhing in the darkness. An overwhelming pressure swells and your bones crack. Your vision fills with blood, but you are denied peace by unconsciousness. Unknown and unseen horrors fill your mind as suffocation grips your throat - until it all stops.

  

An uncanny reflection of yourself stands in front of you. Their eyes empty, and their face hollow. They speak but nothing reaches your ears…

  

You hear a scratching against canvas as you jolt awake. The warm light of your lantern fills your tent. Just a step away you hear a familiar voice call your name. Despite the sun hiding behind the horizon, you are being summoned.

Outside your camp resides a newfound chaos. The shifting mass that is the displaced citizens of Heartwood surrounds you. Comfort is nothing but a distant memory - yet, as the day before, you force yourself to rise…there is work to be done…

Nod calls to you~
