
#Madness #Stagnations-Breath 

The name of the specific fungus that is infecting the [[Great Tree Uhrn]].

Attracted to the dead and vulnerable, the fungus drives its' victims closer to a 'death' state.


***

The spores cannot exist for long on surfaces and are completely hydrophobic.
When direct contact is made, it can be absorbed through the skin but has a significantly lower effect versus inhalation/consumption.

Furthermore, there seems to be a slight steroidal effect that can be exploited, however it puts the user under great physiological and psychological strain.

If an individual were to trek through a spore cloud, mental fortitude and a water soaked cloth over the face would be sufficient protection so long as exposure does not last longer than 2 hours.

Furthermore, the spores are directly tied to anxieties and isolation.
After an incubation period of three days, an individual who is infected will not only display deep anxieties and violent tendencies, they will also begin expelling spores themselves as the infection spreads to their lungs.

After 5 days, their blood will be completely saturated with the spores making it extremely hazardous to handle.
Lastly, after 7 days, their condition is 90% terminal.
After death, it is unknown what occurs to the body as results have remained inconsistent.

***

Those who are inflicted by Stagnation's Breath may receive a +3 Strength Modifier.
