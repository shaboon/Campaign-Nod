Nadir: The Vigilant Ermine 

We were born with knives on our paws and daggers in our mouths. The gods made us strong so that we may rule over the weak. We are powerful. We are carnivores. 

We are weasels. 

There was a sort of "family business" that every weasel in the northern wastes was born into; the illicit meat market. Though outlawed in almost every nook and cranny of the continent, some carnivores and omnivores still clung to the old ideals that the "natural order" must be abided by. Prey and predator living in harmony was a front to nature itself and some groups would see to it that order was restored by any means necessary. Nadir's family was one such group. 

Hailing from the northern winter wastes, the weasel clan found the solitude of the snow to be the perfect place for their home. It was quiet, near inhospitable, and away from prying eyes. It was a perfect place for selling the meat that they had "procured" from their crusades. At night, when nearby villages had all drifted off to sleep, the weasels would quietly sneak into the homes and slaughter the prey animals, taking them back to their home for "cleaning". 

Nadir, once known as a female and by the name "Zenith", never wanted to be a part of this. Though meat was the only thing ever offered to her, she would refuse until she was on the brink of starvation. Having no other option, she would force herself to consume the meat if only for her own survival. Zenith detested and loathed this and begged his family numerous times to stop this inhumane practice, but her cries would fall on deaf ears. 

Zenith would eventually give up on trying to convince them otherwise and resigned herself to her unfortunate fate. Zenith was trained from an early age how to fight. Though she detested the killing of the prey animals, she would absorb all of the teachings of fighting through her father. She quickly became very skilled with the bow and the blade, and was even gifted a wolf claw dagger as passed down through the generations. Her father also bestowed upon her the white sashes of the weasel clan, in honor of the purity of their bloodline. 

Zenith's relationship with her family was strained due to her beliefs, but her father maintained a close relationship with her. Though she loved her father, the cruelty of their family's practices would only ensure that she would never consider this place home. When it finally came to pass that she was old enough to be on her own, she left without saying goodbye and never once looked back. She took with him the dagger and the sashes that were now dyed a brilliant blue for the grief and repentance of the lives that her family stole. 

Zenith, now adopting the name Nadir and transitioning to be non-binary, now wanders the open plains, wooded forests, and thunderous mountains in search of those who would possibly need his protection. He found work most often in traveling caravans and thus saw more of the world than he had ever thought possible in his youth. He met animals from all walks of life; amphibian, avian, mammalian, reptilian, and so many others. Though he was warmed by their presence, he would never become close with any of them for fear of them finding out about his past. 

Now, Nadir finds themselves in a refugee camp, assisting those who have need of his protection, and finds a curious group of folk that have seemingly caught his attention.
