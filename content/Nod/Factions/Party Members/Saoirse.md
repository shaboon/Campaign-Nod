Background:

	As a small child she was taken into the temple and trained to be a religious and cultural facilitator. She has been raised to practice the bovine's belief system to fit into different cultures and guide people into a bountiful life. 

	 She has worked to be the best and strived to contribute back to her community to pay them back for helping her when she was young. She struggles with the death of her parents, only remebering they died due to foul play and she is ashamed to hold a longing for revenge. Knowing that her work to help the good people around her may help her come to peace with wanting the passing of a not so great person.
