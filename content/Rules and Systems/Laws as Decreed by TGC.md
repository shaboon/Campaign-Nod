 Laws and Behavior expected and tolerated as dictated by the population of Nod, and enforced by [[The Greater Chain]]
 
 1. One shall not consume the flesh of another sentient being
	 This includes:
	 1. Mammals of all sizes
	 2. Reptiles of all sizes
	 3. Birds of all sizes
	 4. Amphibians of all sizes
	   
	Should one find that a creature is not included within this list, but feels they qualify as "understood sentience", the beings and/or a representative must stand before the Great Tree Uhrn and make their case before the council members.
		To make such case, the group in question must apply for sponsorship from one of the existing clan representatives.
		
2. One shall not take possessions of which are not their own, unless rightfully claimed and proven to be deserving of said property
	   What is defined as "rightfully claimed" and/or "proven" is delegated to that municipals local governing body
	   - Should appeal be pursued, one must contact their local government who then shall set up a court date of which an agent of [[The Greater Chain]]
