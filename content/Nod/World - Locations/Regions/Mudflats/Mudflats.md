Background:
Mudflats is one of the [[Regions]] of Nod

This region typically experiences slightly more temperate warm and cold cycles throughout the year.

With the growth and separation of [[Clan Chelonii]] as well as [[Clan Lacertilla|Lacerilila]], most of [[Clan Anura|Anura]]’s influence and power have diminished. While a burden when trying to grow and develop their clan, they are surprisingly relevant. With most recent events, nearly all traffic from all other clans has ceased to [[Heartwood]], and yet, with permission from the rest of the council, [[Clan Anura]] has been seen sending their citizens to the flooded city. Recently, their [[Farru|capital]] which was once owned by the Passeri has had runes glow beneath its borders.
